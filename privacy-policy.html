Saves – Privacy Policy and KVKK Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is processed when you use the Saves mobile application (the "App"). Penguin Studio ("we") is the developer of the App and, under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and related legislation, informs you in its capacity as the data controller.

1. Summary

  • Saves does not require you to create an account; we do not ask for contact details such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The links, images, notes, and other content you save are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers and cannot be viewed by us.
  • The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device data, as described below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Links, images, text, and notes you save in the App (including content you share to Saves from other apps) are stored in a local database on your device. This content stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include App data in a device backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy, not by us.

3. Link Previews

When you save a web link, the App connects directly to the relevant website to retrieve preview information (such as the page title, description, and image). As with any web request, the website you link to may receive your IP address and basic device information. This information does not reach us and is not stored by us. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the websites you save.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. AdMob may automatically collect and process the following information to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, settings)
  • App and ad interaction data (e.g. ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance data related to ads

Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can reset your Advertising ID or turn off personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

For more information about how Google uses data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time from within the App.

5. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing Personal Data

We do not collect personal data directly ourselves. Data processed by Google AdMob is used to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and make the App available free of charge.

This data is collected by automated means (through the AdMob SDK integrated into the App) and is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest under Article 5(2)(f) of the KVKK, and, where required for personalized advertising, your explicit consent. For users in the EEA and United Kingdom, personalized advertising is based on explicit consent under the GDPR; non-personalized advertising, security, and fraud prevention are based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your saved content with anyone. Data collected by the AdMob SDK is transferred to Google and, where applicable, Google's advertising partners for the purposes described above. Information may be disclosed to relevant authorities when required by law or in response to a duly authorized request from public authorities.

7. International Data Transfers

Google may process data in countries outside Türkiye, including the United States. Such transfers are carried out under the conditions set out in Article 9 of the KVKK and appropriate safeguards, such as standard contracts used by Google.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Content you save in the App remains on your device until you delete it in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all App data stored on your device. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's retention policies.

9. Data Security

Your saved content is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which is protected by Android's security model. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit.

10. Your Rights Under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you may apply to the data controller to exercise the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data is being processed,
  • To request information about the processing if your personal data has been processed,
  • To learn the purpose of processing your personal data and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose,
  • To know the third parties to whom your personal data is transferred, domestically or abroad,
  • To request correction of your personal data if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • To request deletion or destruction of your personal data under the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK,
  • To request that correction, deletion, or destruction be notified to third parties to whom your personal data has been transferred,
  • To object to a result against you arising from the analysis of processed data exclusively through automated systems,
  • To claim compensation for damage if you suffer loss due to the unlawful processing of your personal data.

Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, most requests can be handled directly on your device (e.g. by deleting content or uninstalling the App). For data processed by Google, you can use your device's ad settings or the Google My Ad Center. You may submit requests to the email address below; your requests will be answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board.

If you live outside Türkiye, you may have similar rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, or Brazil's LGPD; you may exercise these rights using the same contact address.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will take the necessary steps.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
