Saves – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Penguin Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Saves mobile application (the "App"). Penguin Studio is the developer of the App and the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

1. Summary

  • Saves does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details.
  • The links, images, notes and other content you save are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers and we cannot see them.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device data, as described below.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you save a link, image, text or note in the App (including content you share to Saves from other apps), it is stored in a local database on your device. This content stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

If you have Android backup enabled on your device, Android may include App data in your device backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy, not by us.

3. Link Previews

When you save a web link, the App connects directly to that website to retrieve preview information (such as the page title, description and image). As with any web request, the website you are linking to may receive your IP address and basic device/browser information. We do not receive or store this information. Please refer to the privacy policies of the websites you save.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, settings)
  • App interaction and ad interaction data (e.g. ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance data related to ads

Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

For more information on how Google uses data, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from within the App.

5. How We Use Information

We do not collect personal data ourselves. Information processed by Google AdMob is used to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and support the free availability of the App.

6. Legal Bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalized advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and security are based on our legitimate interest in offering the App free of charge and keeping it secure. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your saved content with anyone. Data collected by the AdMob SDK is shared with Google and, where applicable, its advertising partners for the purposes described above. We may disclose information if required by law or by a valid request from public authorities.

8. International Data Transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Google relies on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, for such transfers.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Content you save in the App remains on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored App data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's retention policies.

10. Data Security

Your saved content is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which is protected by Android's security model. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. These rights may arise under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD, and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled directly on your device (for example, by deleting content or uninstalling the App). For data processed by Google, you can use your device's ad settings or Google My Ad Center. You may also contact us using the details below, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: We do not sell personal information. Sharing of identifiers with advertising partners for personalized ads may be considered "sharing" under the CCPA/CPRA; you can opt out by turning off personalized ads in your device settings.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
